A NOTE FOR THE TOUGH DAYS

Megha,
you've got this.

Life hamesha plan ke hisaab se nahi chalti, cutie. Kabhi plans change honge, kuch cheezein work out nahi karengi—and haan, kabhi main bhi tumhare paas na rahun. Par tum apne aap mein kaafi ho; tum sambhal sakti ho.

Rona aaye toh ro lena—par aansuon ko yeh mat samajhne dena ki tum haar gayi. Thoda rukna, saans lena, situation ko practically dekhna: mere haath mein kya hai, aur mera agla chhota step kya ho sakta hai? Phir himmat se saamna karna, zarurat ho toh madad lena, aur dheere-dheere aage badhna. 🫂

Zindagi turns aur challenges degi; har cheez tum control nahi kar sakti, par apna next step choose kar sakti ho. Soft heart rakho, apne liye strong stand lo.

✿

Mujhse ek promise, meri strong girl?
Haar maan-ne ka nahi—apna agla step dhoondh kar aage badhne ka. ♡

✿

Jab sab kuch ek saath lage, bas yahin se shuru karo:

  1. Ruko. Ek aaram ki saans lo. Abhi sab solve karna zaroori nahi.
  2. Jo hua usse aur jo tum assume kar rahi ho, unhe alag-alag socho.
  3. Ek chhota agla step chuno. Zaroorat ho toh kisi bharosemand insaan se help maango.

Practical hona = apni feelings ko sunna, phir apne liye sahi step lena. 🤍

Delhi ♡ Kolkata

Main paas rahun ya door, meri wish wahi hai: tum apne liye himmat se khadi raho.
Tumhara Delhi wala ♡
